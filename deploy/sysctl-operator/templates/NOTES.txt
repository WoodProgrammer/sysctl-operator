1. Check the manager is running:

   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deploy/{{ include "sysctl-operator.fullname" . }}

2. Apply a SysctlProfile (kernel parameters) or ScriptProfile (custom scripts).
   Example — tune sysctls on nodes labelled sysctlstack=tuned:

   cat <<'EOF' | kubectl apply -f -
   apiVersion: sysctl.k8s.io/v1alpha1
   kind: SysctlProfile
   metadata:
     name: network-optimized
     namespace: kube-system
   spec:
     nodeSelector:
       matchLabels:
         sysctlstack: tuned
     sysctls:
       - name: net.core.somaxconn
         value: "65535"
   EOF

3. Watch the rollout. The operator creates an applier DaemonSet, waits until
   every selected node reports success, then tears the DaemonSet down:

   kubectl -n kube-system get sysctlprofile network-optimized \
     -o jsonpath='{.status.nodeStatuses}' | jq
